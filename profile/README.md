## What is Houve?

Houve is a local-first desktop app. You describe what you want in plain language, such as swapping a token or bridging funds between chains. Houve works out the transaction and shows you exactly what will happen before anything is signed.

The AI never touches your keys.

## How it works

1. **Ask.** Say what you want in natural language.
2. **Prepare.** Deterministic code builds the transaction and simulates its effects. The language model only interprets your intent and never builds or signs a transaction.
3. **Approve.** You review the expected asset movements in a separate approval window.
4. **Sign.** Your own wallet signs. Keys stay with you.

## Principles

- **Non-custodial.** Houve never holds or sees your private keys.
- **Local-first.** The app runs on your machine, not on our servers.
- **Verifiable.** Every action is a recorded proposal you can inspect, even if you rejected it.
- **Signed updates.** Updates install only when they carry a manifest signed with our release key and the hash matches.
